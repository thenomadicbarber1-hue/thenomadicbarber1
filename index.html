06 The universal chair language

Show, don’t
just tell.

Save this checklist before any appointment—especially when language or local haircut terms change.

  1. Bring two referencesOne for the shape you want, one for what you do not want.
  2. Name your non-negotiableLength, hairline, beard blend, maintenance, or how it grows out.
  3. Describe your routineProducts, tools, wash schedule, and real styling time.
  4. Confirm before the first cutAsk the barber to repeat the plan and agree on the starting length.
