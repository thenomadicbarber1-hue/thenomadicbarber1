HHotels & hospitality

Your guests ask where to get a haircut. Can your team answer well?

A shop with five-star reviews can still be the wrong fit. Many barbers specialize in certain textures — a guest with coily 4C hair and a guest with straight hair need very different shops. When a recommendation goes wrong, the guest remembers who sent them.

What your team gets

  • A quick reference to hair types 1A–4C, so staff know what to ask
  • A vetted local barber list organized by texture and style
  • Guest-ready language that is respectful and inclusive
  • Red flags to screen out before recommending a shop
  • Branded copies of the guide for VIP rooms and guest gifts
